TradFi Perp Rotation — research dashboard

A rules-based, long-only rotation that holds the ten strongest-trending equities and equity ETFs from Binance's TradFi perpetual menu — ranked on how far each underlying sits above its own 200-day average, capped at two names per theme, rebalanced weekly, and pulled entirely to cash when too few names qualify. Signals are computed on the underlying stocks; the exposure is taken through perps. No leverage.

    Not investment advice. A personal research dashboard for a personal book, not affiliated with any regulated fund and not a recommendation to buy or sell anything. Positions shown are micro-sized verification experiments.
    Backtest disclosure. Weekly Friday-close basis from 2018. The signal is read on the session before the fill — in the backtest that is the next close, in the live book an execution within hours of it — so there is no look-ahead in either, and since Amendment 3 both read the same session. Every figure is net of 10bp round-trip fees, a funding band of 0 to +6%/yr charged on invested days, and forgone dividends. Costs are stressed at 2× and 4×. Long only, no leverage, so loss is bounded by the capital committed.
    Where live departs from the simulation. The live rule blocks buys in names paying more than +30%/yr funding; the simulation has no funding rule at all, because these contracts are too young to have a testable funding history. The live book can therefore sit under-invested in cash where the simulation never did.
    The bias that cannot be removed. The tradeable menu is Binance's own 2025–26 listing selection, so absolute returns are flattered by construction; only the relative comparisons on this page are defensible.
    Data. Underlying prices from yfinance; contract specs, funding and liquidity from Binance's public API; identities verified two-source against Binance's listing announcements. Open source.
